# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straight with people. Argue about the work, not the person. No harassment, no
slurs, no sexualised remarks, no personal attacks, no publishing anyone's private
information. Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith until
they show you otherwise.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any other space that
represents this project.

## Applies to everyone

Maintainers included. A maintainer who is rude to a contributor is a bigger
problem than a contributor who is rude to a maintainer, because we set the tone.

## Not covered by this document

Technical disagreement, blunt review comments, and "this is wrong, here is why"
are all fine and welcome. The voice of this project is terse by design. Terse is
not the same as unkind, and neither is a rejected pull request.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems to build@arrakis.security. Reports go to Arrakis Security
staff and stay private. We will respond, and we will say what we did.

Consequences, in escalating order: a private word, an edited or removed comment, a
temporary block, a permanent block. We will explain which one and why rather than
acting silently.
